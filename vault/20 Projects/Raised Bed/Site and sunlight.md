The strip along the south fence gets direct sun from mid-morning until late afternoon. The corner by the shed loses it around two, when the shed's shadow crosses it. Most fruiting vegetables want six or more hours of direct sun, so the fence strip wins.

> [!caution] Uncertain
> "Six or more hours" is the common rule of thumb on seed packets. It varies by crop, and leafy greens manage with less. Check the packet for whatever goes in next spring.
