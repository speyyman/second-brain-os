Untreated timber boards for the sides, enough for one bed once the length is known. Four corner posts. Exterior screws. Cardboard for the bottom, which smothers the grass and rots away. Topsoil mixed with compost to fill it. Garlic bulbs from a garden centre rather than a supermarket, since supermarket garlic may have been treated to stop it sprouting.
