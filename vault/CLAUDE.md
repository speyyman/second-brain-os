## Privacy, in short

The full rules are in the Privacy section of AGENTS.md, and these hold even before you've read it. Never save sensitive information (health, money, relationships, other people, identity, location, passwords or keys) to any assistant's memory; memory may hold only how the vault works and the owner's style preferences. Don't copy note contents into other documents, connected apps, web searches or published pages unless the owner asks for that specific content to go there. In the notes you write, leave out account numbers, passwords and keys, mention other people as little as possible, and never diagnose or label anyone.
