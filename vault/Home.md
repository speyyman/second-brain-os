> [!info]- How this vault works (click to open)
> The full manual is the [[Vault Guide]]; agents read [[AGENTS]].
>
> **Capturing.** A new note lands in `00 Inbox` as a plain capture. You decide where it goes only during the weekly review.
>
> **Where things go.** Anything with a finish line goes in `20 Projects`. Things you looked up go in `30 Research`, and things from your own head go in `40 Mind`. Ongoing parts of life live in `50 Life`, and anything finished moves to `90 Archive`. If you can't tell, leave it in the Inbox.
>
> **The daily check-in** takes about five minutes: rate your energy from 1 to 5, write a line about yesterday, pick your top three.
>
> **The weekly review** takes twenty minutes, once a week, with a timer. Open this week's review note and follow its five steps.
>
> **The no-guilt rule.** If you miss days, or even a whole week, don't try to catch up. Start again with today.
