# Raised Bed

## Goal
A raised bed of untreated timber, filled and planted with garlic before the first hard frost, so there is something growing over winter.

## Why it matters
Growing something from scratch is the slowest, most forgiving project there is. It doesn't mind being ignored for a week.

## Next action
The length decides how much timber to buy, so it comes first. See [Site and sunlight](site-and-sunlight.md).
